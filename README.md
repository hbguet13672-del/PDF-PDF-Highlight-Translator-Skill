# PDF-PDF-Highlight-Translator-Skill
A minimal AI skill for highlighting research PDFs and adding Chinese translations directly to PDF annotations.
中文介绍

PaperLens 是一个极简的科研论文阅读 Skill。

它让 AI 自动高亮英文论文中的重要内容，并将对应中文翻译写入 PDF 注释，尽量保留原始排版、公式和图片。

无需复杂配置，具体效果取决于运行环境提供的 PDF 编辑能力。

PaperLens is a minimal AI skill for reading academic papers. It highlights important passages and adds Chinese translations directly to PDF annotations, while preserving the original document.

No separate translation document. No complicated workflow. Just a few instructions.

Limitations

PDF annotation appearance may vary between readers.

Scanned or complex PDFs may require additional processing.

Translation accuracy and highlight coverage depend on the AI model and available tools.
